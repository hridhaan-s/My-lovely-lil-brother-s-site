My Hobbies & Interests

My hobbies are an important part of who I am. They allow me to explore my creativity, develop new skills, expand my knowledge, and discover new ideas. I enjoy activities that challenge me to think, learn, imagine, and create. My four main hobbies are coding, reading, book writing, and studying.

Coding

Coding is one of my favourite hobbies because it allows me to turn ideas into real digital projects. I enjoy learning how websites work, experimenting with HTML, and exploring the world of programming. I like building websites, understanding how different elements work together, and discovering how technology can be used to solve problems. Every new project gives me an opportunity to learn something different, improve my logical thinking, and develop my technical skills. I hope to continue exploring web development and other areas of technology as I grow.

Reading

Reading is a hobby that helps me discover new ideas, explore different worlds, and learn about subjects beyond my everyday experiences. I enjoy reading because books can introduce me to interesting characters, fascinating facts, imaginative adventures, and new ways of thinking. Reading also helps me improve my vocabulary, develop my language skills, and understand how writers communicate their ideas. I am especially interested in stories and subjects that encourage curiosity and imagination. Every book offers something new to discover.

Book Writing

Book writing is one of the ways I express my imagination and creativity. I enjoy developing story ideas, creating characters, building fictional worlds, and planning exciting adventures. Writing allows me to transform thoughts and ideas into stories that readers can explore. I have written books such as The Galaxy Escape and The Rescue, which are available on Bribooks, as well as Light Years Before Dinosaurs: The Secret Beneath Delhi, available on Amazon.

Working on books teaches me patience, planning, storytelling, and the importance of revising my work. I hope to keep writing new stories, exploring different ideas, and growing as a young author.

Studying

Studying helps me build my knowledge, understand new concepts, and explore how the world works. I enjoy learning about different subjects and discovering connections between what I learn in school and the world around me. For me, studying is not only about completing schoolwork or preparing for examinations. It is also an opportunity to ask questions, develop critical thinking, strengthen my understanding, and discover topics that interest me.

I believe that learning something new every day can help me improve my skills and become a more knowledgeable person. I try to approach learning with curiosity and an open mind.

These four hobbies reflect my interests in learning, creativity, technology, and storytelling. Whether I am writing a new chapter, reading a book, working on a coding project, or studying a new topic, I enjoy the opportunity to discover something new and develop my abilities.
